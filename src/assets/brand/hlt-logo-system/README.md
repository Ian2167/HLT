## Direction Selected (historic, superseded)

The strongest direction is a refined structural ascent mark: it preserves the original upward HLT concept, keeps the feeling of lift and infrastructure, and replaces the dated pixel field with a small number of deliberate modular support blocks.

## Why This Works

- Keeps recognisable continuity with the Canva source mark.
- Improves small-size clarity by reducing the lower noise.
- Feels more operational and infrastructural than a generic SaaS icon.
- Works as a full logo, app-style icon, favicon, and social mark.
- Keeps the Thai accent controlled by applying violet only to the mark and optional `Thai` wordmark segment.

## Usage Notes

- Use `hlt-primary-dark.svg` on dark navy or black page headers.
- Use `hlt-primary-light.svg` on white or pale surfaces.
- Use `hlt-icon-dark.svg` for favicon, app icon, mobile nav, and small avatars.
- Use `hlt-secondary-stacked-dark.svg` for hero cards, social cards, or presentation covers.
- Use `hlt-monochrome.svg` where single-color reproduction is required.
- Keep the Thai subline separate from the primary logo. It can be used as campaign/supporting text, but not inside the main production lockup.
