## Governing Doctrine

- Start from mechanism, not slogans.
- Explain mechanism before recommendation.
- Keep human texture. Remove damaging friction.
- Adapt for Thai audience by tone and structure, not direct translation.
- Prioritise: clarity, cultural fit, commercial logic, calm authority, human warmth, persuasion.
- Reject hype, guru tone, agency tone, fake urgency, emotional manipulation, and over-polished AI smoothness.
